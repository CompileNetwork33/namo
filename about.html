Our Story

From a Small Retail Pharmacy to a Regional Wholesale Leader

Namo Medical Store was founded in 2009 with a single purpose — to make quality medicines and healthcare products accessible to every household in Pritam Colony, Fatehgarh Sahib. What started as a modest retail counter has grown into one of the most trusted names in both retail pharmacy and wholesale medicine distribution across the region.

Our retail wing serves walk-in customers and home-delivery patients with prescription medicines, OTC drugs, health devices, baby care, and personal care essentials. Our wholesale division supplies clinics, hospitals, nursing homes, and other pharmacies with bulk medicines at competitive prices — backed by full authenticity guarantees.

Over 10,000 families and 200+ institutional partners trust us every month. We are proud to be fully licensed, compliant, and recognised by the regional Drug Control Authority.
